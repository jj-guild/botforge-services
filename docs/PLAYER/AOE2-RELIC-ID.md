# Finding your Relic ID (AoE2)

The bot identifies your account in two ways:

1. **Your in-game name** (exact, case-sensitive) — the bot searches the
   matching profiles and offers the homonyms with clan, country and Elo
   so you can pick yours.
2. **Your Relic ID** — your account's unique numeric id
   (e.g. `17190369`).

## Where to find your Relic ID

On **[aoe2insights.com](https://www.aoe2insights.com)**, sign in with
your Steam account and open your profile. **Under your avatar**, your
Relic ID shows with two icons on its right:

```text
# 17190369 [⧉]
```

- The `#` marks it as an identifier.
- The number (`17190369`) is your **Relic ID**.
- The **copy** button (right) puts it on your clipboard.

Click the displayed id, hit the copy icon, and paste it into the
"Your in-game name or your Relic ID" field of the add-account form.

> **Note** — the id in your page's URL (`/user/12488557/`) is
> aoe2insights' *internal* id, NOT your Relic ID. aoe2insights urls are
> no longer accepted by the bot (the site blocks our requests): always
> use the number under your avatar.
